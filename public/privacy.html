LEDGEr Privacy Policy

Tactical River Catch Log & Offline Map Ledger • Published by BIGGUY_littleboat (David Scott Shetley)

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 • Effective Date: September 30, 2026

🛡️ The Angler's Privacy Principle:

Anglers keep their honey holes and river waypoints sacred. LEDGEr does NOT sell, track, or share your secret fishing spots, catch coordinates, weights, or catch photos with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties. All core records are stored offline-first in your device's local memory (IndexedDB and CacheStorage).

1. Overview & Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the LEDGEr mobile application and web application created by BIGGUY_littleboat. LEDGEr is designed to function in remote river environments and dead zones where cellular networks are unavailable.

2. Data Collected & How It Is Used

Below is the detailed disclosure of what hardware permissions and data types are accessed and why:

Data Type / Permission Purpose & Operational Scope Storage & Transmission
Precise Location
(ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)		 Stamps exact GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, accuracy) onto your catch logs and bait waypoints. Calculates river distances and matches nearby USGS river gauges. Active ONLY when using the app on deck. LOCAL STORAGE
Stored in device IndexedDB. Never tracked in background when app is closed. Never shared with brokers.
Camera & Media
(CAMERA, Media Library)		 Allows the angler to take or select photos of catches for Trophy Baseball Cards and spot identification. Compressed to under 500KB locally. LOCAL DEVICE
Stored in device storage. Processed locally via client-side canvas compressor.
Angler Catch Records
(Species, Bait, Weight, Notes)		 Cataloging your personal fishing ledger, solunar activity, and river patterns. LOCAL FIRST
Stored on your phone. Optional cloud backup sync only when initiated.
Offline Map Tiles
(CacheStorage)		 Downloaded 50-mile satellite navigation tiles cached for offline river navigation. LOCAL CACHE
Can be purged at any time from Settings.

3. Location Data & Background Tracking Disclaimer

In compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies:

  • No Background Location: LEDGEr does not access, record, or transmit your device location while the application is backgrounded or terminated.
  • Graceful Degradation: If you decline location permissions, the app will continue to work. You may manually place pins or input coordinates without granting device GPS permissions.

4. Third-Party Services & Telemetry

To enrich catch records with scientific environmental data, LEDGEr queries the following open telemetry providers:

  • USGS Water Services (United States Geological Survey): Fetches public river streamflow discharge (CFS) and river gauge height (ft) for the nearest monitoring station. No personal data is sent.
  • Open-Meteo: Fetches open meteorological barometric pressure (inHg), wind speed, and ambient temperature based on general river coordinates. No user identifiers or accounts are shared.

5. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion (Your Rights)

You maintain 100% control over all data created in LEDGEr:

  • Export Catch Data: At any time, you can export your entire catch log history into standard .csv (spreadsheet) or .json backup files via the Settings view.
  • Instant Data Deletion: You can completely wipe all catch logs, waypoints, photos, and cached offline map tiles by tapping "Clear All Logs" and "Purge Banked Map Tiles" in the Settings view. This immediately deletes the IndexedDB databases and local cache storage on your device.
  • Assistance: For data inquiries or assistance, you can email DavidScottShetley@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

LEDGEr does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is intended for general audience anglers.

7. Security & Encryption

Network transmissions (such as environmental API requests or cloud backups) utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Local data is stored securely in sandbox-isolated browser storage.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
